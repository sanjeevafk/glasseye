01 · The problem

Glass towers hide their damage in plain sight.

Façade inspection across modern high-rises is fundamentally manual, dangerous, and infrequent. Rope-access teams or one-off drone flights inspect only 1–2× per year — between visits, seal degradation, water ingress, and structural cracks go unnoticed until repair costs multiply.

01

Infrequent Cadence

Checked 1–2× per year; defects wait months before detection while damage multiplies under structural load.

02

High-Risk Rope Access

Human technicians hanging from ropes 50+ stories high face extreme life-safety hazards for routine checks.

03

Sub-Pixel Crack Loss

Standard downscaled vision models crush thin 2–4px hairline cracks into blur before the first convolution.

04

Zero Building Memory

Legacy inspections rarely compare against the building's history — each visit restarts from zero context.

05

No Immutable Audit Log

Subjective paper checklists lack verifiable cryptographic timestamps required for insurance & warranty claims.

06

Escalating Repair Costs

Uncleaned sealant stains and minor surface grime turn into catastrophic multi-lakh panel replacement failures.

02 · The solution

A closed inspection loop, not a one-time scan.

GlassEye turns façade video into a living decision loop: detect the anomaly, understand what it is, decide whether it's safe to act on, act, and re-check the result — all mapped onto a persistent digital twin.

01 · SEE

Candidate Extraction

640px YOLOv8 proposes defect candidate regions from drone survey video feeds.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Semantic Labeling

Classifies surface grime, sealant damage, or structural fracture with severity scoring.

03 · DECIDE

Deterministic Policy

Rule engine routes surface issues to cleaning and structural cracks to human engineering review.

04 · ACT

Simulated Actuation

Dispatches honest simulated actuator commands (SIMULATED_COMPLETE) with human modal confirmation.

05 · VERIFY

Closed Reinspection

Post-remediation flight re-checks panel coordinate to confirm RESOLVED or maintain ESCALATED.

05 · Engineering narrative

From 0% Baseline to 61.10% Drone Defect Recall.

A 6-phase journey transitioning from procedural synthetic scaffolding to multi-domain 640px vision models and SAHI sliding-window tiling evaluated on 200 building-disjoint aerial drone holdouts.

640px Native Resolution

Retrained YOLOv8 at 640px native on Colab Tesla T4 GPU (50 epochs, AdamW), lifting BFDD mAP50-95 from 0.1127 to 0.1648 (+46.2%).

Sliding-window 480px patch slicing with cross-tile NMS prevents thin hairline cracks (2–4px wide) from being crushed by downsampling.
