SYSTEM TOPOLOGY & DECISION PIPELINE

GlassEye Autonomous Facade Inspection Loop

A deterministic inspection-to-verification architecture: fine-tuned YOLO perception, spatial panel tracking, side-effect-free policy decisions, simulated remediation, and closed-loop re-inspection verification.

KEY DIFFERENTIATOR

Closed-Loop Verification

Unlike standard inspection tools that stop at detection, GlassEye completes the remediation lifecycle.

  • Simulates cleaning only for validated surface issues (dirt, stain).
  • Re-inspects identical facade coordinates using real YOLO inference.
  • Labels outcome strictly as RESOLVED, UNRESOLVED, or ESCALATED.

SAFETY & TRUST

Side-Effect-Free Policy

Deterministic rules govern action paths; LLM vision acts only as a guarded advisory layer.

  • Structural defects (cracks, spalls) automatically bypass to human review.
  • Advisory VLM inspects ambiguous crops before clean execution.
  • VLM timeouts or errors fail safely to human escalation, never action.

AUDIT & INTERFACE

Evented 3D Digital Twin

Append-only event log projected into an interactive Three.js panel map.

  • Every state transition recorded in immutable events.jsonl.
  • Full mission replay reproducing exact historical inspections.
  • 4×3 panel grid with real-time SSE streaming to the frontend.
